# ADR Campus v0.4 — Propose, Review, and Decide

Version 0.4 turns a private architectural decision draft into a shared proposal and allows an active maintainer to accept or reject it. It covers the journeys defined by [Propose a Decision](../stories/02-propose-a-decision.md) and [Review and Decide](../stories/03-review-and-decide.md).

The story documents remain authoritative. This release record defines their delivery boundary and links the implementation backlog without restating every acceptance criterion.

## Release Outcomes

At the end of v0.4:

- an active author can validate and preview the exact draft revision they intend to propose;
- confirmation atomically changes that ADR from `Draft` to `Proposed` without changing its stable identity;
- proposed content is immutable and visible to every active organization member;
- an active maintainer can review the exact persisted proposal and confirm acceptance or rejection;
- acceptance records an optional note and makes the ADR a current organizational decision;
- rejection requires a reason and preserves the ADR as shared history rather than current architecture;
- proposal and decision operations are atomic, idempotent, and safe under concurrent attempts; and
- every lifecycle state is displayed without implying authority it does not possess.

## Included Stories

### Propose a Decision

The current active author may propose a complete draft. Proposal validates and normalizes all narrative sections, freezes the confirmed content, records proposer and proposal time, and moves the existing ADR into organization-visible shared storage.

The confirmation is tied to the exact draft version previewed. A stale confirmation, unauthorized actor, incomplete draft, invalid narrative section, or uncertain persistence outcome cannot create a partial or duplicate proposal.

### Review and Decide

An active maintainer may accept or reject a proposal, including one they authored. The decision is tied to the exact proposal reviewed and rechecks maintainer authority when confirmation is committed.

Acceptance permits an optional valid note. Rejection requires a valid reason. The first successfully persisted outcome is final; concurrent or repeated attempts cannot overwrite it or create duplicate decision history.

## Storage and Authority Boundary

- Private `Draft` content belongs to the Workbench.
- `Proposed`, `Accepted`, and `Rejected` records belong to the organization-visible Library.
- Lifecycle, attribution, and decision evidence must be durable and suitable for the Archive even where the first implementation commits the projections together.
- Keycloak remains authoritative for current Member and Maintainer roles.
- Page visibility is not authorization; every proposal and decision command rechecks current authority when persisted.

## Deliberately Deferred

Version 0.4 does not include:

- general search, filtering, sorting, or pagination across the shared record;
- comments, discussion, reviewer assignment, voting, or notifications;
- proposal withdrawal, amendment, or resubmission;
- reversal or reopening of a decision;
- reconsideration of a rejected proposal;
- supersession of an accepted ADR; or
- draft recovery and organization-administration completion.

The minimal Proposed, Accepted, and Rejected views required to complete these journeys are included. The complete discovery experience remains scheduled for v0.5.

## Definition of Done

Version 0.4 is complete when:

1. all in-scope acceptance criteria from stories 02 and 03 pass automated tests;
2. proposal and decision transitions pass retry and concurrency tests;
3. member, maintainer, author, and non-member visibility rules pass authorization tests;
4. the browser supports preview-and-confirm flows without changing state on cancellation;
5. proposed and decided records remain durable across application restart;
6. proposed and decided content cannot be edited through any route or command;
7. a manual QA run carries one draft through proposal, acceptance, and a separate proposal through rejection; and
8. the full solution builds without new warnings or errors.

The ordered implementation work and QA checkpoints are recorded in the [v0.4 backlog](../backlog/v0.4.md).

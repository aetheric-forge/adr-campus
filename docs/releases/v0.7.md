# ADR Campus v0.7 — Complete Organization Administration

Version 0.7 completes the application-owned administration behavior around the SSO authority already used by ADR Campus. It delivers the remaining work from [Administer the Organization](../stories/06-administer-the-organization.md) and the draft recovery and expiration behavior defined by [Draft a Decision](../stories/01-draft-a-decision.md).

The story documents remain authoritative. This release record defines the delivery boundary and links the implementation backlog without restating every acceptance criterion.

## Existing Foundation

ADR Campus already authenticates through Keycloak, derives the current Member and Maintainer roles from configured groups, fails closed when current authority cannot be established, and presents a member-visible roster. Version 0.7 preserves that SSO control-plane boundary and adds only the durable local state needed for organization settings, observed transitions, historical attribution, and private-draft recovery.

## Release Outcomes

At the end of v0.7:

- valid deployment configuration initializes one stable organization without granting first-visitor authority or mutating SSO;
- active members see a current, safe roster derived from the configured groups, while protected mutations continue to recheck sufficiently current SSO authority;
- ADR Campus idempotently records effective membership, maintainer, and identity-property changes that it observes;
- active maintainers can rename the organization without changing its stable identity, SSO mappings, ADR ownership, or decision history;
- membership loss starts one 30-day recovery window for each private draft still owned by the departed member;
- a returning member resumes an unreassigned, unexpired draft, while completed reassignment and expiration remain final;
- active maintainers can list eligible drafts and atomically reassign one to a current active member without exposing its content through shared discovery;
- unreassigned drafts become inaccessible when their recovery window ends and are eventually purged by an idempotent worker dispatched through the Maintenance institution;
- active maintainers can read application-owned administration history, while ordinary members cannot see recovery or administrative metadata; and
- loss of the final active maintainer is detected and reported without elevating another member or bypassing SSO.

## State and Consistency Boundary

- Keycloak remains authoritative for identity, enabled state, organization membership, and Maintainer assignment.
- ADR Campus never creates, deletes, enables, disables, or changes an SSO identity or group.
- The local organization and membership projection records only the effective state and history required by ADR lifecycle behavior.
- Reprocessing the same observed directory state creates no duplicate transition, recovery deadline, or administration event.
- A membership transition and all draft-recovery effects caused by it become visible together or not at all.
- Reassignment rechecks current Maintainer authority, the recipient's current membership, the recovery deadline, and the exact draft version at commit time.
- Expiry affects access at the deadline even before physical purge; purge is a later storage-maintenance operation and cannot restore visibility.
- The Maintenance institution governs job contracts and run outcomes; its background facility collects Post Office commands and dispatches bounded workers.
- Postal acceptance records custody of a maintenance request, not successful execution; correlated run state records processing outcomes.
- Historical actions remain attributed through stable external identity references even after membership or identifying properties change.

## Deliberately Deferred

Version 0.7 does not include:

- SSO user or group mutation, invitations, enrollment, or an ADR Campus user-management system;
- multiple organizations, teams, departments, or custom application roles;
- organization deletion or SSO group remapping through the application;
- restoration of expired drafts or reversal of completed draft reassignment;
- editing or deleting application-administration history;
- reproducing the SSO control plane's own audit log; or
- notifications about membership, recovery, or expiration events.

## Definition of Done

Version 0.7 is complete when:

1. every applicable acceptance criterion from stories 01 and 06 passes automated or documented manual QA;
2. bootstrap accepts only resolvable configured groups with at least one active Maintainer who is also a Member and never grants first-visitor authority;
3. repeated observation of unchanged SSO state is idempotent and changed state produces exactly one effective transition and its coordinated recovery effects;
4. current authority is rechecked for protected mutations and unavailable authority fails closed without persisting a domain change;
5. organization rename validation, normalization, concurrency, authorization, and audit behavior pass automated tests;
6. membership loss, return, Maintainer changes, identifying-property changes, and final-Maintainer loss preserve coherent projections and history;
7. recovery listing and reassignment protect draft content, require current authority, preserve draft identity and content, and record attribution;
8. expiry removes every web path to an unreassigned draft at the deadline and a postal Maintenance job purges it idempotently with observable run outcomes;
9. roster, settings, recovery, and administration-history views expose only the information allowed for the current role; and
10. the full solution test suite, solution build, persistence restart verification, background-maintenance verification, and `git diff --check` pass.

The ordered implementation work and QA checkpoints are recorded in the [v0.7 backlog](../backlog/v0.7.md).
